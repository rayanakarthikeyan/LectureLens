How LectureLens works

  1. Import a lecture. Add an audio file, a slide PDF, and an SRT or VTT transcript. You can add just one file, but combining them gives the best experience.
  2. Explore the timeline. Click a timestamp to play from that moment. Select a slide number beside a passage to link the two.
  3. Search with evidence. Every result points to transcript text or a slide page. The app makes no ungrounded answers.

Offline transcription is available for 16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV when Qualcomm's Whisper sample or whisper.cpp is configured. See README.md for setup. The app does not use a cloud API.
